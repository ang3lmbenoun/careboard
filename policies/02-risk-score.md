---
title: How the care risk score works
visibility: public
source: CareBoard documentation (illustrative rules, not clinical guidance)
---
The care risk score adds points from simple rules. A latest A1C of 9 or higher adds 3 points, and 8 to 8.9 adds 1 point. No A1C on record, or one older than 180 days for type 2 diabetes (365 days for other conditions), adds 2 points. An A1C that rose by 0.5 points or more since the previous reading adds 1 point.

Other rules cover a stopped GLP-1 medicine, a newly started insulin, and weight gain. A total of 3 or more points is High, 2 is Medium, and anything lower is Low.

The score is illustrative and is not clinical guidance.
