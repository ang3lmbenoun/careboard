At Clinic 1, a latest A1C of 9.0 or higher triggers a follow up call to the patient during the same week. The care coordinator logs the call and offers the next available appointment.

If the patient cannot be reached after two attempts on different days, the coordinator sends a letter and flags the patient for the next clinic review.
