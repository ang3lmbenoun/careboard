---
title: Medicaid and Medicare in plain words
visibility: public
source: CareBoard documentation (general summary)
---
Medicaid is a joint federal and state program that covers people with low income. Medicare is a federal program that mainly covers people aged 65 and over and some people with disabilities. Medicare Part D covers prescription drugs.

In CareBoard, patient coverage is one of Medicaid, Medicare, Dual (both) or Commercial. Coverage is synthetic and assigned by a simple rule based on age.
