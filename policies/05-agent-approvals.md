---
title: What the care agent may and may not do
visibility: public
source: CareBoard documentation
---
The care agent runs as the signed-in role and can read only what that role can read. It can propose a follow up call list, which waits for the person to approve or reject it. Approval records the decision and sends nothing outside the app.

The agent never sees names or birth dates, never edits patient records, and never sends messages outside the app. An administrator can switch all agents off.
